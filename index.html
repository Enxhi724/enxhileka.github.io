Enxhi Leka

Post-doctoral researcher in Economics

I am currently a Post-doctoral researcher at Institut Mines-Télécom Business School (IMT-BS), working with the MEDEI project. I earned my PhD in Economics from IMT-BS in June 2025, under the supervision of Professor Grazia Cecere.

I hold a Master’s Degree in Economics (Master IREN) from Université Paris-Saclay, a joint program with Université Paris Dauphine, École Polytechnique, CentraleSupélec, and Télécom Paris, specializing in Digital Economics and Quantitative Research.

My research lies at the intersection of digital economics, regulation, and data infrastructure. I use empirical and quantitative methods to study the economic impacts of data regulation, including how policies shape data flows, data center locations, the economics of the Internet backbone, and spatial competition among cloud service providers.
